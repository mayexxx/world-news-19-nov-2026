ICJ finds Myanmar committed genocide

In a 9–6 ruling, the court cited evidence of about 10,000 killed in the 2017 Rohingya crackdown. In its first such finding against a state in 80 years, it ordered refugees’ safe return and compensation.

The game unlocks at midnight local time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The standard edition costs $79.99 and needs 212 GB; there is no PC version at launch.

Houthi missile hits Riyadh tower

Windows were blown out across 7–8 floors of the 80-storey, 385 m PIF Tower, Saudi Arabia’s wealth fund headquarters. No injuries were reported. An Aramco building was hit Nov 8.

Cyclone Akvan nears India

Landfall is expected by noon Friday between Narsapur and Puri, with winds of 90–100 km/h, gusting to 110. About 50,000 evacuated in Odisha; schools shut in six districts.

Anthropic makes record IPO debut

The AI company raised $81.3 billion, the largest IPO ever, at $83 a share and about a $1.5 trillion valuation. Shares begin trading Thursday on Nasdaq as ANT.

Four killed in Bangladesh unrest

Muhammad Yunus’s ancestral home was burned in Chattogram and Grameen Bank branches attacked. Dhaka police arrested 565 ahead of an Awami League nationwide lockdown.
